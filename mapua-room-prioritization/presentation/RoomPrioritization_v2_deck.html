MAPÚA UNIVERSITY · ROOM UTILIZATION · SESSION 2

Too many sections, too few of the right rooms

Every term, more course sections need a face-to-face room than our campuses can hold. Who gets a room should follow policy, not first come, first served.

Stakeholders

Students and faculty

Problem

Limited physical rooms across campuses

Input data

Curriculum, courses, rooms, enrolled students

Output

A ranked view of courses for room priority

THE IMPROVED SOLUTION · V2

Rank every offering, then book real rooms and times

1

Forecast demand

Per course and campus. Only students who passed the prerequisites count.

2

Score priority

Six weighted factors: graduating share, bottleneck, needs a lab, demand, required, scarcity.

3

Timetable

Mon–Sat 3-hour blocks. Right room type, campus and size. No double-booking.

4

View and report

Ranked view, section schedule, room timetable, KPIs, shareable report.
