Walk the four steps left to right. Step 1 is where v2 got smarter: demand is per campus and checks prerequisites. Step 2 is the policy: weights live in a settings file the Registrar controls. Step 3 is new: a real timetable, so no room is ever double-booked. The fallback chain is the policy for what happens when rooms run out: courses that can go online do that before anyone is pushed to evenings; labs that can't go online get evening or Saturday slots; anything left is flagged with a reason.
